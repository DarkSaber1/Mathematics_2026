
# Exercise 1 – Matrix Size and Entries

## Problem Statement

Find the dimensions, specified entries, and requested row and column vectors of matrices A and B.

## Solution

Given:

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### 1. Matrix Dimensions

- A has 2 rows and 3 columns: $2 \times 3$.
- B has 3 rows and 2 columns: $3 \times 2$.

### 2. Matrix Entries

$$
a_{12}=-1,\quad a_{23}=5
$$

$$
b_{21}=-2,\quad b_{32}=1
$$

### 3. Row and Column Vectors

Second row of A:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}
$$

First column of B:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\\4\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Explanation

Matrix dimensions are determined by the number of rows and columns. The notation $a_{ij}$ identifies the entry in row $i$ and column $j$.

## Verification

All selected entries and vectors match their positions in the original matrices.

## Final Answer

- Dimensions: A = $2 \times 3$, B = $3 \times 2$
- Entries: $-1,\ 5,\ -2,\ 1$
- Second row of A: $(0,4,5)$
- First column of B: $(1,-2,4)^T$
